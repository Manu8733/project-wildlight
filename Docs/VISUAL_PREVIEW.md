# Original asset preview

These are **Blender asset showcases, not Unreal gameplay screenshots**. They show original geometry/materials authored for Wildlight, composed and rendered in the cloud. They use no generated stock image or downloaded reference-game assets.

The latest direction is cozy rounded construction, cream plaster, warm terracotta roofs, sage vegetation and a restrained magical evening atmosphere. The complete original kit includes 19 building prototypes, 12 static unit prototypes and 10 environment/decorative meshes; final animation, import, collision and in-engine graphical acceptance remain pending.

# Gameplay VFX and audio briefs

These are authoring parameters and acceptance rules. Niagara remains unverified authoring work. Original deterministic audio synthesis studies are generated by `Tools/author_audio.py` under `Art/Audio/`; they are prototypes pending listening, mixing and gameplay integration, not a finished soundtrack. Execute on the real engine/GPU and record per-effect cost before final integration.

## Niagara briefs

| System | Gameplay trigger | Low-tier authoring target | Readability requirement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `NS_FireflyPocket` | Night in safe scenery pockets | ≤24 particles per visible pocket; 3 pockets initially | Never confused with selected units or projectiles |
| `NS_BeaconPulse` | Connected-node state and resource pulse | One short-lived pulse plus contained glow | Link direction remains visible with particles off |
| `NS_NetworkBreak` | Connection transitions from on to off | Single 0.4 s retracting pulse | Persistent state shown by mesh/HUD, no repeated flashes |
| `NS_RootBarrier` | Accepted root command | Footprint-first growth over 0.5 s; limited leaf motes | Solid route-blocking boundary visible from above |
| `NS_LightBridge` | Accepted legal bridge command | Anchor thread, resolved surface, late warning | Span and expiry shown when decorative VFX disabled |
| `NS_Mend` | Actual healing tick | Small upward motes, 0.6 s | Does not resemble damaging projectile |
| `NS_ShadowVeil` | Active veil area | Ground-level edge mask, restrained center | Ownership and selected allies remain readable |
| `NS_SiegeImpact` | Simulation-confirmed impact | Brief dust and debris; pooled | No heavy persistent screen obstruction |

Expose intensity, particle cap, lifetime and quality scale. Pool repeated effects. Bind events to stable IDs and state transitions; cleanup when an entity disappears or a match resets. A Low setting removes ambient particles first, preserving route and target cues.

## Audio event map

| Event | Intended cue | Acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Select/confirm | Soft wood/glass click | Fast feedback without masking alerts |
| Invalid command | Quiet dry tick plus reason text | Not punitive or excessively loud |
| Cargo delivery | Small basket/coin action | Rate-limited in dense economies |
| Construction complete | Warm short chord/wood settle | Distinct from recruitment |
| Light disconnect | Low glass interval | Clear event, not repeatedly spammed |
| Beacon reconnect | Rising contained resonance | Matches actual restored state |
| Bridge/root cast | Different material character | Spell identity with sound only |
| Attack/siege | Weapon/material response | Tied to actual hit, spatialized |
| Victory/defeat | Restrained authored stinger | Clear match outcome |
| Forest/river ambience | Layered wind, foliage, water | Ducks during essential alerts |

Use separate master/music/effects/ambience/UI volume settings. Avoid voice acknowledgements until a consistent original actor/source can be recorded or licensed. Do not claim a final composed score from specifications or a generated oscillator placeholder. For each actual sound include source, method, author and rights in the asset registry, then verify its integration and mix on the laptop.
